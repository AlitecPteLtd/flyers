# Flyer Content — Project Site Procurement

## Cover / Top Section

**Title:** Project Site Procurement

**Subtitle:** Material Requisition to Purchase, Receipt & Project Cost Control

**Introduction:**
Alitec's project site procurement solution takes every material request through a controlled approval chain — Draft to PM Reviewed to Approved to PO Issued — then on to purchasing, vendor price comparison, site delivery, and verified billing. Every requisition and purchase order line is tagged by project, division, and cost code, all inside one Odoo platform.

## Quick Benefits

1. **Controlled MR Approval** — Draft to PO Issued approval chain with a full audit trail.
2. **Project Cost Traceability** — Project, division & account codes on every requisition line.
3. **Faster Purchase-to-Pay** — One-click MR-to-PO generation with vendor price comparison.
4. **Site Access Control** — Users see and transact only at their assigned project locations.

## Key Capabilities

1. **MR Approval Workflow** — Draft, Submitted, PM Reviewed, Approved & PO Issued with sign-off.
2. **Project & Cost Codes** — Tag requisition and PO lines by project, division, and account code.
3. **Back-Charge Recovery** — Flag material costs to recover from subcontractors or cost centers.
4. **MR → RFQ/PO Automation** — Generate purchase orders directly from approved requisitions.
5. **Vendor Price Comparison** — Compare vendor quotes and history before confirming a PO.
6. **Site Location Control** — Restrict stock users to only their assigned project locations.
7. **Branded PO & MR PDFs** — Cost-coded purchase orders, duplicate copies, and MR print forms.
8. **Bill Verification & Reporting** — Bill-verified checkpoint plus purchase line reporting by cost code.

## Business Workflow

1. **Draft MR** — Site team drafts a requisition with project, cost code, and location of use.
2. **PM Review** — Project manager reviews and approves or sends the MR back for correction.
3. **Approve & Sign** — Authorized approver signs off; approval date and user are captured.
4. **Purchase & Compare** — Generate RFQ/PO from the MR; compare vendor prices before confirming.
5. **Receive & Verify** — Goods received at the project site; bill matched and verified before payment.

**Control modes:**
- MR Status (Draft, Submitted, PM Reviewed, Approved, PO Issued)
- Cost Coding (project, division, account code)
- Back-Charge (cost recovery from subcontractors/cost centers)
- Site Location (restricted to assigned project locations)
- Bill Verified (manual verification flag before payment)

**Additional features:**
- Up to 3 supporting attachments on every MR
- Submitted vendor quotation upload on the PO
- Delivery order upload at goods receipt
- Vendor invoice upload on the bill
- Approver e-signature capture on the PO
- Duplicate PO copy for site or store use
- Auto-captured vendor price history
- Read-only protection on vendor contact data
