## Quick Benefits (4)

1. **Structured Catalog** — Group, type, form, and series taxonomy for fast, consistent lookup. / **结构化目录** — 分组、类型、形态与系列分类，实现快速一致的查找。
2. **Serial Range Assign** — Assign existing serials by range or pasted list on the delivery line. / **序列号范围分配** — 在送货明细行上按范围或粘贴清单分配现有序列号。
3. **Confident Barcode Picking** — Every demand line shows in the scanner; overscanning is blocked. / **可靠条码拣货** — 每条需求行都显示在扫描界面；超扫将被自动拦截。
4. **Warranty & Repair Tracking** — Helpdesk tickets record serials, faults, and replacement stock. / **保修与维修跟踪** — 服务台工单记录序列号、故障与更换库存。

## Key Capabilities (8)

1. **Product Taxonomy** — Item Group, Type, Form, and Series fields classify every kit and instrument. / **产品分类体系**
2. **Serial Range Assign** — Pick first/last serial or paste a list to assign existing serials on delivery lines. / **序列号范围分配**
3. **Barcode Mixed Picking** — Placeholder lines surface every demand; an overscan guard blocks wrong counts. / **混合条码拣货**
4. **Auto-Pick on Validate** — Validating a delivery marks reserved lines picked, avoiding stray split backorders. / **校验自动拣货**
5. **Large Picking Search** — Search bar and 100-line paging make big multi-line kit deliveries easy to scan. / **大单拣货搜索**
6. **PO Final Location & Dropship** — Purchase lines default to warehouse stock or clear automatically for dropship orders. / **采购最终库位与直运**
7. **Branded Documents** — Company-branded quotations, invoices, and delivery slips with dropship addressing. / **品牌单据**
8. **Warranty & Repair Desk** — Helpdesk tickets capture serial, purchase source, fault, solution, and replacement. / **保修维修服务台**
