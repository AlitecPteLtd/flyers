# Flyer Content — Investment Holding Accounting

## Cover / Top Section

**Title:** Investment Holding Accounting

**Subtitle:** Multi-Currency Deposits, Securities & Equity Control with Odoo

**Introduction:**
Alitec's investment holding accounting solution helps holding and treasury entities track time deposits,
investment and marketable securities positions, and share capital movements — while keeping multi-currency
balances reconciled, related-party dealings governed, and a full finance print pack ready for stakeholders
— all inside one Odoo platform.

## Quick Benefits

1. **Time Deposit Control** — Book placements with rate, tenor and due-date tracking on every ledger entry.
2. **Holdings & Securities** — Investment and marketable securities ledgers with fair-value movement history.
3. **Share Capital Registry** — Issue and buyback transactions roll up to per-member registry balances.
4. **Multi-Currency Control** — Balance reports, revaluation and ageing with foreign-currency totals.

## Key Capabilities

1. **Time Deposit Ledger** — Interest rate, start and due date linked to journal entries and a dedicated report.
2. **Investment Holdings** — Addition, disposal, write-off and adjustment types with functional-currency cost.
3. **Marketable Securities FV** — Purchase, disposal and fair-value adjustment postings with price history.
4. **Share Capital & Registry** — Issue/buyback transactions and member-level share registry balances.
5. **Multi-Currency Balance & Reval** — Currency and partner balance reports plus automated revaluation adjustment.
6. **Related Party Governance** — Related-party tagging on partners, move-line reason codes and overdraft limits.
7. **Payment-On-Behalf** — Dedicated on-behalf payment reference with smart partner filters at register-payment.
8. **Finance Print Pack** — Invoice, debit note, journal voucher and delivery-invoice PDFs with QR and PO ref.

## Business Workflow

1. **Place & Book** — Book time deposit, investment or securities entry with rate and currency.
2. **Post & Link** — Journal entries link to the deposit, investment or securities reference.
3. **Settle on Behalf** — Payment-on-behalf and multi-currency reconciliation across related parties.
4. **Revalue & Report** — Month-end FX revaluation, ageing totals and share registry rollup.
5. **Print & Distribute** — Branded invoice, debit note, JV and payment-advice pack issued.

**Control modes:**
- Time deposit rate/tenor/due-date fields
- Investment addition/disposal/write-off/adjustment types
- Securities purchase/disposal/fair-value adjustment types
- Share registry addition/disposal per member
- Related-party governance tag on partners

**Additional features:**
- Currency & partner balance reports
- Automated FX revaluation with adjustment
- Aged balance with FX currency totals
- Move-line reason codes for audit trail
- Account overdraft limit tracking
- Payment-on-behalf reference on journal items
- QR code and PO reference on invoices
- No-signature company print option
