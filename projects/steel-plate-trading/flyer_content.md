# Flyer Content — Steel Plate Trading

## Cover / Top Section

**Title:** Steel Plate Trading

**Subtitle:** Dimensional Quoting, Timed Delivery & Weight-Based Operations for Steel Stockists

**Introduction:**
Alitec's steel plate trading solution helps stockists quote by length, width and thickness,
auto-calculate shipped weight from material density, and price confidently by the ton. Sales and
purchase teams reuse verified customer and supplier price history, confirm a fixed delivery time
window, and issue branded quotations, delivery orders, invoices and packing lists in kilograms —
while keeping external sales channels isolated and exporting AR/AP data straight to MYOB.

## Quick Benefits

1. **Dimensional Quoting** — Quote by L×W×T with instant density-based weight and ton pricing.
2. **Verified Price Books** — Customer and supplier prices are logged and reused automatically.
3. **Timed Delivery** — Confirm firm delivery dates with a fixed time-slot window.
4. **MYOB-Ready Accounts** — Branded documents plus one-click AR/AP export to MYOB.

## Key Capabilities

1. **L×W×T Dimension Capture** — Length, width and thickness flow from order lines into every shipment.
2. **Density-Based Weight** — Shipped weight auto-calculates from dimensions and material density.
3. **Automatic Ton Pricing** — Cost per weight converts to a live price-per-ton on every product.
4. **Customer & Supplier Price Books** — Confirmed order and invoice prices are logged and reused per partner.
5. **Timed Delivery Scheduling** — Delivery date plus a fixed end-time window on orders and invoices.
6. **External Sales Isolation** — External teams see only their own customers, leads and orders.
7. **Branded Docs & Packing List** — Quotations, DOs, invoices and packing lists print total weight in kg.
8. **MYOB AR/AP Export** — Tab-delimited AR/AP export mapped to MYOB tax codes.

## Business Workflow

1. **Quote & Measure** — Sales quotes by L×W×T; weight and ton price calculate instantly.
2. **Confirm & Price** — Order confirmed; customer/supplier price book updates automatically.
3. **Schedule Delivery** — Confirm delivery date and a fixed time-slot window for the yard.
4. **Pack & Ship** — Delivery order and packing list print with total weight in kilograms.
5. **Invoice & Export** — Branded invoice issued; AR/AP entries exported to MYOB.

**Control modes:**
- Dimensions flow from order lines into stock moves
- Live price-per-ton computed from cost and weight
- Price books sync automatically from confirmed SO/PO/invoices
- External sales team sees only its own partners and orders
- AR/AP entries mapped to MYOB tax codes for import
