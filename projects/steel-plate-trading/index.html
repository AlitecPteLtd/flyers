Steel Plate
Trading

Dimensional Quoting, Timed Delivery & Weight-Based Operations for Steel Stockists

Alitec's steel plate trading solution helps stockists quote by length, width and thickness, auto-calculate shipped weight from material density, and price confidently by the ton. Sales and purchase teams reuse verified customer and supplier price history, confirm a fixed delivery time window, and issue branded quotations, delivery orders, invoices and packing lists in kilograms — while keeping external sales channels isolated and exporting AR/AP data straight to MYOB.
