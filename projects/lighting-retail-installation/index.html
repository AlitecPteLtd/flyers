Lighting Retail &
Installation Operations

Quote Fixtures, Control Discounts, Schedule Site Jobs & Bill with Commissions

Alitec's lighting retail and installation solution helps fixture showrooms quote with real product photos, sell down discontinued stock safely, and enforce VIP/Gold/Silver discount ceilings by sales role. Confirmed orders turn into scheduled site appointments and Gantt-ready project tasks, technicians close jobs with FSM worksheets, and invoices compute staff and interior-designer commissions automatically — all in one Odoo platform.

Visual Quotes

Product photos on every order line for accurate fixture selection.

Controlled Discounts

VIP, Gold & Silver tier limits enforced automatically by sales group.

Site Scheduling

Confirmed orders auto-create appointments and Gantt project tasks.

Commission Payouts

Staff and interior-designer commissions computed on every invoice.
