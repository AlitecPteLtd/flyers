# Flyer Content — Lighting Retail & Installation Operations

## Cover / Top Section

**Title:** Lighting Retail & Installation Operations

**Subtitle:** Quote Fixtures, Control Discounts, Schedule Site Jobs & Bill with Commissions

**Introduction:**
Alitec's lighting retail and installation solution helps fixture showrooms quote with real product photos, sell down discontinued stock safely, and enforce VIP/Gold/Silver discount ceilings by sales role. Confirmed orders turn into scheduled site appointments and Gantt-ready project tasks, technicians close jobs with FSM worksheets, and invoices compute staff and interior-designer commissions automatically — all in one Odoo platform.

## Quick Benefits

1. **Visual Quotes** — Product photos on every order line for accurate fixture selection.
2. **Controlled Discounts** — VIP, Gold & Silver tier limits enforced automatically by sales group.
3. **Site Scheduling** — Confirmed orders auto-create appointments and Gantt project tasks.
4. **Commission Payouts** — Staff and interior-designer commissions computed on every invoice.

## Key Capabilities

1. **Visual Order Lines** — Product photos display directly on quotation and order lines.
2. **Discontinued Sell-Down** — Locks ordering to free stock so phase-out fixtures never oversell.
3. **VIP/Gold/Silver Tiers** — One-click discount buttons capped by each tier's approved limit.
4. **Price Guardrails** — Group-based ceilings on line discount, unit price cuts and fixed amounts.
5. **SO → Project & Tasks** — Confirmed orders sync analytics to linked project tasks automatically.
6. **Site Appointments** — Appointment date, time window and customer set from the order date.
7. **Gantt Planning** — Custom "By Project" board with stage-colored pills and fast task lookup.
8. **FSM Worksheets** — Field worksheets prefill job data; invoices compute staff & ID commission.

## Business Workflow

1. **Quote Fixtures** — Sales rep builds SO with product photos and stock-checked lines.
2. **Apply Tier Discount** — VIP/Gold/Silver wizard applies group-limited discount.
3. **Confirm & Schedule** — SO confirm creates project task, appointment slot and Gantt entry.
4. **Site Visit & FSM** — Technician completes job via FSM worksheet with signature.
5. **Invoice & Commission** — Invoice posts with staff and ID commission computed automatically.
