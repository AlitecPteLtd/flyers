# Flyer Content — Air-Conditioning Service Operations

## Cover / Top Section

**Title:** Air-Conditioning Service Operations

**Subtitle:** From Quotation and On-Site Appointments to Worksheets, Delivery & Invoicing with Odoo

**Introduction:**
Alitec's air-conditioning service solution helps HVAC teams capture service site and appointment details on every task, turn confirmed sales orders into linked projects with shared analytics, and guide technicians through an HVAC worksheet checklist — closing each job with a signed service chit, branded documents, and clear price and cost visibility.

## Quick Benefits

1. **Site & Appointment** — Service address, contact and appointment window captured on every task.
2. **Order-to-Project** — Confirmed sales orders generate linked projects, tasks and analytics.
3. **Signed Service Chit** — Job completion PDF with site photos and technician/client signatures.
4. **Price & Cost Control** — Role-based visibility shows unit cost next to price on order lines.

## Key Capabilities

1. **Service Site & Appointment** — Address, contact and appointment window captured directly on the task.
2. **Order-to-Task Automation** — Confirmed sales orders create linked project tasks with a shared analytic account.
3. **HVAC Service Checklist** — Built-in filters, fan and motor checklist seeds every worksheet.
4. **Service Chit PDF** — Completion report lists parts used with photos and signature blocks.
5. **Drag & Drop Photos** — Technicians drop job photos straight onto worksheet image fields.
6. **Commission by Resource** — Reporting splits order value across the resource assigned to each task.
7. **Branded Documents** — Quotations, invoices, delivery orders and statements carry consistent branding.
8. **Price & Cost Visibility** — Role-based access shows unit cost next to price for authorized users.

## Business Workflow

1. **Quote & Confirm** — Quote air-conditioning service and confirm the sales order.
2. **Schedule Visit** — Task captures service address, contact and appointment window.
3. **Complete Worksheet** — Technician runs the HVAC checklist and attaches job photos.
4. **Sign-Off Chit** — Print service chit with photos and technician/client signatures.
5. **Deliver & Invoice** — Branded delivery, invoice and reporting close out the job.

**Worksheet controls:**
- Service address, contact and resource on the task
- Appointment start/end window on schedule and kanban
- Default filters/fan/motor checklist
- Image drag-and-drop on worksheet fields
- Technician and client signatures on the chit

**Additional features:**
- Linked project and task share one analytic account
- One-year service project planning window
- Parts and quantities listed on the service chit
- Multiple job photos per completion report
- Reference unit cost beside price on order lines
- Cost and price fields restricted by user role
- Commission-for-resource list and pivot reporting
- Branded invoices, delivery orders and statements

**引言：**
Alitec 空调服务解决方案帮助 HVAC 团队在每个任务上记录服务地址与预约信息，将已确认的销售订单转化为共享分析账户的关联项目，并引导技术人员完成空调专属检查表工单——以带照片和签字的服务单收尾，配合统一品牌单据与清晰的价格成本可视化。

**快速收益：**
1. 现场与预约 — 服务地址、联系人与预约时间段记录在每个任务上
2. 订单转项目 — 确认销售订单自动生成关联项目、任务与分析账户
3. 签字服务单 — 工作完成 PDF 附现场照片及技术员/客户签名
4. 价格成本管控 — 按角色权限在订单行显示单位成本对比价格

**业务收益：**
1. 现场作业更高效 — 清晰的服务地址与预约信息减少技术员沟通成本
2. 照片留证更可靠 — 拖拽上传的照片与签名为每个工单提供有力凭证
3. 作业记录更规范 — 统一的空调检查表与服务单适用于每次预约
4. 提成分配更公平 — 按人员分摊的报表让技术人员薪酬更加透明
