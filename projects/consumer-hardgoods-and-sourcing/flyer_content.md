# Flyer Content — Consumer Hardgoods & Sourcing

## Cover / Top Section

**Title:** Consumer Hardgoods & Sourcing

**Subtitle:** Agent and Trade Modes — Merchandising, Factory Audits, QA Gates & Ocean Logistics with Odoo

**Introduction:**
Alitec's consumer hardgoods sourcing and trading solution runs Agent commission orders and Trade principal orders in one Odoo platform. Merchandise by sector, family, and sub-family with partner SKU and season mapping, gate every order through PPS, PPT, PSI, and OK QA checkpoints, track ocean freight from loading port to ETD, and screen factories for social, technical, and environmental compliance including FSC and EUDR.

## Quick Benefits

1. **Agent & Trade Modes** — Run commission-based agent orders and principal trade orders side by side.
2. **QA Gate Control** — PPS, PPT, PSI, and OK QA checkpoints track every order line to release.
3. **Ocean Logistics** — Ports, FCL/LCL, forwarders, and ETD targets tracked to actual shipment.
4. **Factory Compliance** — Social, technical, and environmental audits plus FSC and EUDR screening.

## Key Capabilities

1. **Agent & Trade Order Modes** — Set order mode per sale order; agent mode requires a client commission line to confirm.
2. **Dual Commission Engine** — Compute and refresh client and supplier commission lines with one click.
3. **Merchandising Taxonomy** — Classify products by Sector, Family, and Sub-Family for a clean catalog structure.
4. **Partner SKU & Season** — Map customer SKU codes and season or promotion tags to internal products.
5. **Production QA Gates** — PPS, artwork, PPT, and PSI checkpoints record passed, failed, or capped results.
6. **Ocean Freight Tracking** — Loading and discharge ports, FCL/LCL, container count, and forwarder on every order.
7. **Factory Audit Register** — Social, technical, and environmental audits with expiry status plus FSC/EUDR flags.
8. **Delay Reason Analytics** — Internal and external delay codes roll up to milestone and service-level reporting.

## Business Workflow

1. **Order & Mode** — Sales books the order as Agent or Trade and computes commission lines.
2. **Merchandise & Confirm** — Line tagged by sector, family, sub-family, partner SKU, and season.
3. **Production & QA** — PPS, artwork, PPT, and PSI checkpoints tracked through to OK QA.
4. **Load & Ship** — Container loaded, SO released, and ETD tracked through to shipped.
5. **Invoice & Report** — Commission invoiced, delay reasons logged, milestones reported.

**Order milestones:**
- OK QA — Quality sign-off clears the order for release
- SO Released — Order confirmed and ready for container loading
- Loaded — Container loaded and packed at the origin port
- Shipped — ETD forecast confirms vessel departure
- Commission — Agent and supplier commission settled and invoiced

**Additional features:**
- Origin country and HS code on order lines
- Volume (CBM) and gross weight per line
- Vendor status: active, inactive, or blacklist
- PSI target date vs actual date tracking
- Delay reason codes: internal vs external
- Hardgoods material, colour, carton & dimensions
- FSC and EUDR compliance flags on factories
- Sale line analytics: PI, shipment, commission, delay
