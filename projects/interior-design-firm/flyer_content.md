# Flyer Content — Interior Design & Renovation Operations

## Cover / Top Section

**Title:** Interior Design & Renovation Operations

**Subtitle:** From Design Preference and Product Selection to Quotation, Delivery, Handover & Job Costing with Odoo

**Introduction:**
Alitec's interior design & renovation solution captures each project's renovation type, design preference, and key collection details in CRM, lets signed-in homeowners browse a login-gated product catalogue, and lets designers turn selections into quotations with tracked revisions and a deliberate Manual Accept. Per-line delivery addresses, analytic job costing, signed handover/rectification PDFs, and Singapore e-invoice field readiness keep every renovation on track — all in one Odoo platform.

## Quick Benefits

1. **CRM Design Capture** — Renovation type, design preference & key collection from first enquiry.
2. **Login-Gated Catalogue** — Product selections open only to signed-in homeowners, not the public.
3. **Manual Accept Quoting** — Designer-led quotations with revisions confirmed by deliberate approval.
4. **Job Costing Visibility** — Analytic accounts track each renovation as Ongoing or Closed.

## Key Capabilities

1. **Renovation & Design Intake** — CRM lead captures renovation type, design preference, and key collection.
2. **Login-Gated Catalogue** — Shop pages redirect anonymous visitors to sign in before browsing.
3. **Quoting & Revisions** — Designers quote from selections; one click adds a numbered revision.
4. **Manual Accept Control** — Confirm button relabelled so designers approve orders deliberately.
5. **Per-Line Delivery Address** — Each order line can ship to its own site or unit address.
6. **Job Costing: Ongoing/Closed** — Analytic account tracks status, completion date, and job balance.
7. **Handover & Rectification PDFs** — Printed handover and rectification forms with signature capture.
8. **SG e-Invoice Field Readiness** — Peppol ID and invoice line detail fields prepared, ready for rollout.

## Business Workflow

1. **Capture Lead** — CRM records renovation type, design preference, and key collection.
2. **Login & Browse** — Homeowner signs in to browse the login-gated product catalogue.
3. **Quote & Revise** — Designer builds the quotation and issues numbered revisions.
4. **Accept & Deliver** — Manual Accept confirms the order; delivery split by line address.
5. **Handover & Cost** — Signed handover/rectification PDFs close the job with job costing.

**Control modes:**
- Design fields (renovation type, design preference) on the CRM lead
- Gated shop requiring sign-in before browsing
- Auto-numbered quotation revisions
- Manual Accept in place of default confirm
- Delivery address set per order line

**Additional features:**
- Attention contact on quotations & invoices
- Key collection register linked to each lead
- Handover signature, signed-by name & date
- Rectification photo & work-done checklist
- Per-line address feeds delivery picking
- Completion date required to close a job
- Peppol ID & scheme fields on partners
- Structured e-invoice line detail fields
