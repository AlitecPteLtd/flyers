Installation Field
Service Operations

Appointments, installers, and Gantt planning in one flow

Alitec's installation field service solution helps contractors run sales into project and FSM appointments, assign overall and line installers, plan jobs on a Gantt with linked task slots and popovers, and deliver with installed-by accountability on pickings — while team-scoped documents, cost/price visibility, customer statements, field service printouts, and optional payroll totals to accounting journals close the loop.

Appointment Windows

Date and start/end times captured on every installation task.

Installer Accountability

Overall and line installers, plus installed-by on deliveries.

Team-Scoped Documents

Resource teams see only their field service documents.

Planning Gantt Clarity

Slots linked to tasks with job and customer popover detail.
