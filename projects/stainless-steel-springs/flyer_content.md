# Flyer Content — Stainless Steel Spring Manufacturing

## Cover / Top Section

**Title:** Stainless Steel Spring Manufacturing

**Subtitle:** MTO Production — Engineered Quotes, Shop Floor QC & Packing Labels with Odoo

**Introduction:**
Alitec's stainless steel spring manufacturing solution helps precision spring makers quote multiple order quantities in one click, keep full engineering specs on every part, trace every work order back to its originating sales line, split production by delivery date, and enforce pass/fail quantities on the shop floor — all in one Odoo platform, finishing with packing labels and a complete industrial document set.

## Quick Benefits

1. **Multi-Qty Quotes** — Quote 100 / 500 / 1,000+ pcs in one click using pricelist breaks.
2. **Full Spring Specs** — OD, wire diameter, spring rate and max load on every product.
3. **SO-MO Traceability** — Every work order keeps an explicit link to its sales order line.
4. **Pass/Fail QC** — Capture exact failed and passed quantities at each check.

## Key Capabilities

1. **Spring Master Data** — OD, ID, wire diameter, free length, spring rate, load and torque specs on every product.
2. **Multi-Qty Quote Builder** — One click adds quantity-break lines using pricelist rules and templates.
3. **SO-MO Explicit Link** — Work orders trace back to the sales line even when the MTO chain breaks.
4. **Date-Driven Order Splits** — Lines with different delivery dates auto-group into separate MOs.
5. **Work Order Qty Split** — To-do vs produced quantity auto-creates backorders and next operations.
6. **Pass/Fail Qty Capture** — Quality checks record failed and passed quantities at each checkpoint.
7. **Packing & Label Printing** — Ad-hoc or project labels with PO number, part name and pack quantity.
8. **Industrial Document Suite** — Branded quotations, delivery slips, invoices, debit notes and proforma.

## Business Workflow

1. **Quote Multi-Qty** — Sales builds quantity-break quotations using spring specs and pricelist.
2. **Confirm & Split** — Confirmed lines group by delivery date into traceable manufacturing orders.
3. **Form & Cut** — Work orders route through forming, cutting and shim operations.
4. **QC Check** — Shop floor captures pass/fail quantities at each checkpoint.
5. **Pack & Ship** — Labels print with PO, part and pack quantity before delivery.

**Control modes:**
- Reusable quantity-break templates for instant quotes
- Full spec sheet on quotes and work orders
- Work order to-do/produced qty auto-split
- Fail/pass quantity wizard with auto-computed pass qty
- Ad-hoc or Project label type by picking configuration

**Additional features:**
- Explicit SO line link keeps traceability when MTO chain breaks
- Per-line commitment dates drive procurement grouping
- Historical purchase orders can be manually locked from editing
- Customer reference auto-fill from partner ledger
- Engineering drawing PDFs attached to sale lines, viewable from the MO
- Configurable packing type and quantity per pack on every order
- Dedicated account managers assigned per customer record
- Direct report-to-printer routing for shop floor documents
