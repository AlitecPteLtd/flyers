# Flyer Content — Precision Engineering Commercial Operations

## Cover / Top Section

**Title:** Precision Engineering Commercial Operations

**Subtitle:** Quote, Deliver, and GST-Invoice Engineered Goods with Certification-Ready Documents

**Introduction:**
Alitec's precision engineering commercial operations solution helps machining and fabrication businesses quote and confirm orders on one document, issue GST-ready tax invoices, and print certification-ready quotations, delivery notes, and goods receipt notes carrying company letterhead and quality/safety marks — with multi-company print routing and clean foreign-currency billing, all inside Odoo.

## Quick Benefits

1. **Certified Letterhead** — Branded PDFs with ISO/BizSAFE-style quality and safety marks.
2. **Quote to Order** — One print path switches Quotation to Order Confirmation on approval.
3. **GST Tax Invoice** — Dedicated tax-invoice format ready for GST-registered billing.
4. **Delivery & GRN** — Signed delivery notes and goods receipt notes for every move.

## Key Capabilities

1. **Cert-Ready Layout** — ISO/BizSAFE-style logos on quotes, invoices, and delivery PDFs.
2. **Quotation / OC** — Title auto-switches from Quotation to Order Confirmation by state.
3. **GST Tax Invoice** — Bill-to/ship-to, payment terms, and item numbers on a tax invoice.
4. **Sale Description on Moves** — Order-line notes and specs carry through to warehouse documents.
5. **Item Numbering** — Consistent line numbers from quote through invoice and delivery.
6. **Delivery / GRN Suite** — Outgoing delivery notes and incoming goods receipt notes with signatures.
7. **Multi-Company Print** — Each operating company prints its own letterhead style automatically.
8. **FX Tax Hide** — Foreign-currency invoices hide local-currency tax totals by default.

## Business Workflow

1. **Quote Job** — Sales issues a certified quotation with numbered engineered line items.
2. **Confirm Order** — Same document reprints as Order Confirmation once approved.
3. **Produce & Pick** — Sale description carries onto stock moves for shop-floor context.
4. **Deliver & Receive** — Delivery notes and goods receipt notes print with signature blocks.
5. **Invoice GST** — Tax invoice bills the job with correct currency and tax handling.

**Control modes:**
- Certification logos appear when configured on the company
- Print layout follows the selling or shipping company automatically
- Computed line numbers stay aligned across every document
- Local tax block hidden by default on foreign-currency invoices
- Acknowledgement block for goods receipt on incoming pickings

**Additional features:**
- Quote-to / Bill-to / Ship-to address blocks
- Pro-forma quotation and invoice variants
- Customer remarks and payment terms on print
- QR payment image on the primary invoice layout
- Separate delivery slip with ordered vs delivered quantity
- Auto-print of delivery slip on validation
- Kit/BOM line sections on delivery documents
- Fax and registry details on letterhead
