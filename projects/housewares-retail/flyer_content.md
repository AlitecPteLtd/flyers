# Flyer Content — Housewares Retail

## Cover / Top Section

**Title:** Housewares Retail

**Subtitle:** POS & Trade Sales — Receipts, Pricing History & Statements with Odoo

**Introduction:**
Alitec's housewares retail solution helps linen and housewares stores sell on the shop floor with branded POS tax invoices and cash-drawer hardware, quote trade customers with historical pricing, print professional quotations and delivery notes, and collect receivables with Statement of Account follow-up — all in one Odoo platform.

## Quick Benefits

1. **Shop-Floor POS** — Fast checkout with branded tax-invoice receipts for walk-in buyers.
2. **Hardware Ready** — Receipt printer and cash drawer via POSAgent proxy control.
3. **Trade Price History** — See and reuse past customer prices on sales order lines.
4. **Clear Collections** — Statement of Account and follow-up with printed-date tracking.

## Key Capabilities

1. **POS Tax Invoice Receipt** — Branded receipt with company, GST/VAT, ref, date, and customer.
2. **Cash Drawer Control** — Open drawer from product screen; POSAgent cash-drawer enablement.
3. **Refund Tools** — Refund quantity indicator and clear-all refund lines for cashiers.
4. **Default Walk-in Customer** — Faster POS sessions with a configured default customer.
5. **Sale Price History** — Historical unit prices and discounts for the same commercial partner.
6. **Branded Sales Documents** — Quotation acceptance block, invoices, and delivery notes.
7. **Statement of Account** — Follow-up titled as SOA with partner printed-date control.
8. **Stock Location Moves** — Bulk moves across warehouse locations for retail replenishment.

## Business Workflow

1. **Ring Sale** — Cashier sells housewares and linen at POS with optional default customer.
2. **Print & Cash** — Tax-invoice receipt prints; cash drawer opens via hardware proxy.
3. **Correct Refunds** — Use refund qty view and clear refund lines when adjusting tickets.
4. **Trade Quote** — Sales desk quotes trade accounts using historical line prices.
5. **Deliver & Invoice** — Branded delivery notes and invoices; SOA follow-up for credit terms.

**Control modes:**
- POS receipt as tax invoice
- Printer and cash-drawer hardware
- Refund indicator and clear ticket
- Price history including quotations
- Statement printed-date on partners

**Additional features:**
- Payment information block on invoices
- Product images on sale PDFs
- Smart warnings on documents
- Direct report-to-printer routing
- XLSX partner statements
- Unit price precision on quotations
